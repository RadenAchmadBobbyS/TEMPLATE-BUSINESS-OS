# Publish

Publishing your website to the edge.

Publishing a website triggers a deployment. In Business OS, a `Deployment` record is created, and the active `Page` records are compiled. Traffic is then routed to the published versions.

### How to Publish

1. Open the [Website Builder](/docs/builder).
2. Ensure all changes are saved as a `PageVersion`.
3. Click the **Publish** button in the top right header.
4. Confirm the deployment.

Your site will be live instantly across the global CDN.
